Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 30, 2026  |  Last Updated: June 30, 2026

Chervix Studios ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Bingo Caller mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information.

By using Bingo Caller, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Bingo Caller is designed to work fully offline for gameplay. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not directly collect your name, email address, or any personally identifying information.

However, the App uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain technical information, described below.

2. Third-Party Services

Service Purpose Data Collected
Google AdMob Displaying banner and interstitial advertisements Advertising ID, device information, approximate location (country/region level), ad interaction data
Firebase Crashlytics Detecting and diagnosing app crashes Device model, OS version, crash logs, app version
Firebase Analytics Understanding general app usage patterns App opens, screen views, session duration, device type

These services are provided by Google LLC. Google's own privacy practices govern how this data is further processed. You can review Google's policy here: policies.google.com/privacy

3. Data Stored On Your Device

Your selected app theme and basic game progress (such as games played count) are stored locally on your device using Android's DataStore. This information is never transmitted to us or any third party and is automatically deleted if you uninstall the App.

4. Advertising

Bingo Caller displays advertisements through Google AdMob to support free access to the App. These ads may be personalized based on your interests, using your device's Advertising ID. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization"

5. Children's Privacy

Bingo Caller is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can take appropriate action.

6. Data Retention

We do not operate our own servers that store user data. Data collected by AdMob and Firebase is retained according to Google's own data retention policies, which are generally limited to periods necessary for the stated purposes (advertising, crash reporting, and analytics).

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete information associated with your device. Since we do not directly collect personal data, requests regarding advertising or analytics data should generally be directed to Google via the Google Ads Settings linked above. You may also contact us directly with any questions or concerns.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
